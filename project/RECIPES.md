## Bietjie Lam Groente

*House recipe* · database id `lamb-potjie-chunky-veg`

- **Type:** Potjie
- **Pot:** Falkirk No. 8B flat-bottom
- **Feeds:** 6–8
- **On the coals:** 2½ h
- **Heat:** Low coals

> Bone-in lamb braised with red wine, rosemary and bay, under big chunks of potato, carrot, butternut, patty pans and baby marrow, finished with brown onion soup and fresh parsley. Built together from a Lamb and Mutton SA competition-winning potjie, with herb amounts guided by Taste, Daily Maverick and Demand Africa lamb potjies. Keep all the veg chunky so it holds its shape.

### Ingredients

**Meat (brown first)**
- 2 Tbsp oil
- 2 kg lamb potjie/stewing meat, bone-in
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, chopped
- 250 g brown mushrooms (1 pack), chopped
- 125 ml dry red wine
- 2 ripe tomatoes, chopped
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 375 ml beef stock
- 3 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and black pepper

**Hard veg**
- 750 g baby potatoes
- 6 carrots, in big chunks
- 500 g butternut (1 small), peeled, in big chunks

**Soft veg (goes in later)**
- 250 g patty pans, halved
- 250 g baby marrows, in thick chunks
- 250 g brown mushrooms (1 pack), whole

**Finish**
- 1 packet Knorr Brown Onion soup (you may not need it all)
- 30 g fresh parsley, chopped

**On the side**
- 2 cups basmati or jasmine rice
- 3 cups water
- 1 tsp salt

### Method

1. Season the meat with salt and pepper and brown it in the oil in batches. Take it out.
2. Fry the onion, garlic and chopped mushrooms until soft.
3. Put the meat back, then add the wine, tomatoes, sugar, stock, rosemary and bay leaves. Bring to the boil.
4. Lid on, low coals, and simmer about 1½ hours, until the meat is nearly tender.
5. Pack the potatoes, carrots and butternut on top. Moenie roer nie.
6. After about 30 minutes, add the patty pans, baby marrows and whole mushrooms on top.
7. Cook about 30 minutes more, until everything is tender but still holds its shape.
8. Meanwhile, rinse the rice until the water runs clear. Boil it with the water and salt, then cover and cook on low for 12–15 minutes. Let it stand 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.
9. Take out the rosemary stalks and bay leaves. Sprinkle the brown onion soup powder straight into the pot, a little at a time, letting each bit simmer in before adding more, until the sauce is as thick as you like.
10. Scatter the parsley over and serve on the rice.

## Greek Mint Chicken Sosaties

*House recipe* · database id `greek-mint-chicken-sosaties`

- **Type:** Braai
- **Pot:** none (braai)
- **Feeds:** 6
- **On the coals:** 10–12 min
- **Heat:** Medium-hot coals
- **Makes:** 12 sosaties (app dropdown scales to 4, 6, 12, 24)

> Chicken breast marinated in yoghurt, lemon, mint, oregano and garlic, threaded with red onion, peppers and cherry tomatoes. Half the marinade is kept back before the chicken goes in, so there's plenty of clean sauce to brush on and pour over. Marinate at least 2 hours, or overnight. Built together from Drizzle and Dip, Daily Maverick, RecipeTin Eats and Rock Recipes.

### Ingredients

**Chicken**
- 1.2 kg skinless chicken breast fillets, cut into 3 cm cubes

**Marinade and sauce (half for marinating, half for the sauce)**
- 250 ml plain Greek yoghurt
- 125 ml olive oil
- Zest and juice of 2 lemons
- 6 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 handfuls fresh mint (about 30 g), finely chopped
- 4 Tbsp fresh oregano, chopped (or 4 tsp dried)
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**On the skewer**
- 2 red onions, in wedges
- 1 green pepper, in chunks
- 1 red pepper, in chunks
- 250 g cherry tomatoes (1 punnet)
- 12 sosatie sticks

**To serve**
- 1 lemon, in wedges

**Mint tzatziki (optional extra)**
- 250 g plain Greek yoghurt
- ½ English cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 Tbsp fresh mint, finely chopped
- Juice of ¼ lemon
- Salt and pepper

### Method

1. Mix all the marinade ingredients. Pour half into a separate bowl, cover and refrigerate. This is your sauce.
2. Coat the chicken in the other half, cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight.
3. Soak the sosatie sticks in water for 30 minutes so they don't burn.
4. Thread the chicken onto the sticks with red onion, green pepper, red pepper and cherry tomatoes in between. Keep the chicken pieces the same size so they cook evenly.
5. Take the sauce out of the fridge about 30 minutes before braaiing so it isn't ice cold. If you want it warm, heat it gently at the edge of the fire, but don't let it boil or the yoghurt will split.
6. Optional: make the tzatziki. Squeeze as much water as you can out of the grated cucumber, then mix it with the other tzatziki ingredients and season to taste.
7. Braai over medium-hot coals for 10–12 minutes, turning often, until the chicken is just cooked through and charred at the edges. Breast dries out fast, so take it off as soon as it's done.
8. In the last 2–3 minutes, brush the sosaties with some of the sauce, using a clean brush.
9. Serve with the rest of the sauce poured over, lemon wedges on the side, and the tzatziki if you made it.

## Oxtail Potjie with Red Wine

*Example recipe (placeholder, can be edited or deleted)* · database id `example-oxtail`

- **Type:** Potjie
- **Pot:** No. 3
- **Feeds:** 8
- **On the coals:** 4 h
- **Heat:** Low coals

> Example recipe to show how the book works. Edit it into your own or delete it. Oxtail wants time: keep the coals low and resist lifting the lid.

### Ingredients

**Meat (brown first)**
- 2 kg oxtail, cut into pieces
- 2 Tbsp oil
- 2 onions, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 Tbsp smoked paprika
- 500 ml dry red wine
- 500 ml beef stock
- 2 bay leaves

**Hard veg**
- 6 carrots, thickly sliced
- 6 baby potatoes, halved
- 1 butternut, cubed

**Soft veg**
- 250 g brown mushrooms
- 1 punnet baby marrows, halved

**Finish**
- 2 Tbsp chutney
- 1 Tbsp cornflour mixed with a little water
- Salt and black pepper

### Method

1. Heat the oil in the pot over hot coals and brown the oxtail in batches. Take it out.
2. Fry the onion and garlic until soft, add the paprika, then return the meat.
3. Add the wine, stock and bay leaves. Lid on, pull back to low coals and simmer for about 3 hours. Add a little stock if it gets dry.
4. Pack the carrots, potatoes and butternut on top of the meat. Don't stir.
5. After 30 minutes add the mushrooms and baby marrows as the top layer.
6. Cook another 20–30 minutes until the veg is tender.
7. Stir in the chutney and cornflour paste, season, and serve with rice or potbrood.

## Simple Potbrood

*Example recipe (placeholder, can be edited or deleted)* · database id `example-potbrood`

- **Type:** Potbrood
- **Pot:** No. 10 flat-bottom
- **Feeds:** 8
- **On the coals:** 1 h
- **Heat:** Coals under, coals on lid

> Example recipe. The classic bread for mopping up potjie sauce. Bake it in a flat-bottomed pot while the potjie finishes.

### Ingredients

**Ingredients**
- 1 kg cake flour
- 10 g instant yeast
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 2 Tbsp oil
- About 650 ml lukewarm water
- Butter for the pot

### Method

1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and sugar. Add the oil and enough water for a soft dough.
2. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth. Cover and let it rise in a warm spot until doubled, about 1 hour.
3. Knock it down, shape it, and put it in the buttered pot. Let it rise again for 30 minutes.
4. Set the pot on a few coals and put coals on the lid. More heat on top than below.
5. Bake for about 1 hour. It's done when it sounds hollow when you tap it.
