# Cooking preferences

What the user likes and how they cook, gathered while building PotjieBook. Use this when drafting or adapting recipes.

## Equipment
| Pot | Size | Feeds | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Falkirk No. 8B flat-bottom** (default) | About 35 cm wide × 13 cm deep, roughly 10–12 L (estimated from size, not measured) | 12–16 | Potbrood, and potjies where meat needs room to brown in one layer. Shallow, so layers are thinner and veg cooks a bit faster. |
| **Falkirk No. 3 three-leg** | About 7.8 L (standard No. 3) | 8–10 | Classic deep, layered potjiekos |

Photos of both pots are in `docs/pots/`.

## How the user cooks
- **Thickener:** a packet soup, usually **Knorr Brown Onion**, sometimes **Cream of Mushroom**. Sprinkled **dry, straight into the pot, a little at a time**, letting each bit simmer in, stopping when it's thick enough. Never mixed with water first. Brown onion suits dark lamb, wine and tomato sauces best.
- **Meat:** general potjie/stew meat (bone-in), not only premium cuts like shanks.
- **Veg:** big and chunky so it holds its shape. Hard veg goes on top of the meat, and soft veg (patty pans, baby marrows, whole mushrooms) goes in later.
- **Mushrooms:** split between two stages. Half chopped and fried with the onion at the start, half whole and added with the soft veg.
- **Wine:** less rather than more. 125 ml was enough for a 2 kg lamb potjie.
- **Herbs:** fresh rosemary, bay leaves and parsley in lamb, with woody herbs in early and removed before thickening, and parsley at the end. Fresh mint and oregano for Greek-style chicken.
- **Bacon:** removed from the lamb potjie.
- **Sides:** rice (basmati or jasmine, 2 cups for 6) with potjies. For sosaties, no sides in the recipe itself.
- **Sauce:** likes a decent amount of sauce on sosaties. Done safely by keeping half the marinade aside before the raw meat goes in.
- **Chicken for the braai:** breast, cooked short and taken off as soon as it's done.
- **Chilli:** left out of the sosaties.

## Shopping
- Shops at **Checkers**, using the **Sixty60** app.
- Thinks in packs (2 × 250 g mushrooms, 1 punnet cherry tomatoes, 1 small butternut ≈ 500 g), so write amounts that way where it fits.

## Names and style
- Likes Afrikaans word play in recipe names. *Bietjie Lam Groente* works because "lam" is both lamb and lazy.
- Typical group size is about 6 people.

## How recipes get made
1. The user asks for a recipe ("the best lamb potjie you can find", "Greek mint chicken sosatie").
2. Claude researches several versions online and offers one combined draft with sources, plus a few choices to decide.
3. The user changes ingredients, amounts and method; Claude updates the draft each time.
4. The user may ask for the complete recipe to check.
5. **Only when the user says it's ready** does it go into PotjieBook.
6. Later tweaks to saved recipes are made in place.
