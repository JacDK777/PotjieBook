# Bietjie Lam Groente

**Type:** Potjie · **Pot:** Falkirk No. 8B flat-bottom · **Feeds:** 6–8 · **On the coals:** 2½ h · **Heat:** Low coals

> Bone-in lamb braised with red wine, rosemary and bay, under big chunks of potato, carrot, butternut, patty pans and baby marrow, finished with brown onion soup and fresh parsley. Built together from a Lamb and Mutton SA competition-winning potjie, with herb amounts guided by Taste, Daily Maverick and Demand Africa lamb potjies. Keep all the veg chunky so it holds its shape.

## Ingredients

### Meat (brown first)
- 2 Tbsp oil
- 2 kg lamb potjie/stewing meat, bone-in
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, chopped
- 250 g brown mushrooms, halved
- 125 ml dry red wine
- 2 ripe tomatoes, chopped
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 375 ml beef stock
- 3 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and black pepper

### Hard veg
- 750 g baby potatoes
- 6 carrots, in big chunks
- 1 medium butternut, peeled, in big chunks

### Soft veg (goes in later)
- 250 g patty pans, halved
- 250 g baby marrows, in thick chunks

### Finish
- 1 packet Knorr Brown Onion soup (you may not need it all)
- 30 g fresh parsley, chopped

### On the side
- 2 cups basmati or jasmine rice
- 3 cups water
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. Season the meat with salt and pepper and brown it in the oil in batches. Take it out.
2. Fry the onion, garlic and mushrooms until soft.
3. Put the meat back, then add the wine, tomatoes, sugar, stock, rosemary and bay leaves. Bring to the boil.
4. Lid on, low coals, and simmer about 1½ hours, until the meat is nearly tender.
5. Pack the potatoes, carrots and butternut on top. Moenie roer nie.
6. After about 30 minutes, add the patty pans and baby marrows on top.
7. Cook about 30 minutes more, until everything is tender but still holds its shape.
8. Meanwhile, rinse the rice until the water runs clear. Boil it with the water and salt, then cover and cook on low for 12–15 minutes. Let it stand 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.
9. Take out the rosemary stalks and bay leaves. Sprinkle the brown onion soup powder straight into the pot, a little at a time, letting each bit simmer in before adding more, until the sauce is as thick as you like.
10. Scatter the parsley over and serve on the rice.

*Smaaklike ete!*
