# Simple Potbrood

*Example recipe*

**Type:** Potbrood · **Pot:** No. 10 flat-bottom · **Feeds:** 8 · **On the coals:** 1 h · **Heat:** Coals under, coals on lid

> Example recipe. The classic bread for mopping up potjie sauce. Bake it in a flat-bottomed pot while the potjie finishes.

## Ingredients

### Ingredients
- 1 kg cake flour
- 10 g instant yeast
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 2 Tbsp oil
- About 650 ml lukewarm water
- Butter for the pot

## Method

1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and sugar. Add the oil and enough water for a soft dough.
2. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth. Cover and let it rise in a warm spot until doubled, about 1 hour.
3. Knock it down, shape it, and put it in the buttered pot. Let it rise again for 30 minutes.
4. Set the pot on a few coals and put coals on the lid. More heat on top than below.
5. Bake for about 1 hour. It's done when it sounds hollow when you tap it.
